Common questions

How do I book a sitter?
Create a request with your date, times, your children's age groups and your hourly rate. Sitters in your area can then respond, and you can message them before confirming.

How do payments work?
Payments are processed securely by Stripe. Sitters receive payouts directly to the account they connect during sign-up.

How do I change my password?
Open Settings inside the app and choose Change Password. If you cannot sign in, use the Forgot Password link on the login screen.

How do I delete my account?
Email us from the address on your account and we will delete it, along with your personal data, within 30 days.
