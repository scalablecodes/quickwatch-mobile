Privacy Policy

Quick Watch — last updated 30 August 2026

This policy explains what personal data Quick Watch collects, why we collect it, and the rights you have over it. It applies to everyone who uses the Quick Watch mobile app, whether you are seeking care or offering it.

Who we are

Quick Watch is operated by Mac Daniel Ugwu. For any privacy question, or to exercise the rights described below, contact REPLACE_WITH_YOUR_SUPPORT_EMAIL.

What we collect and why

DataWhy we collect it
Name, email address, phone number, date of birthTo create and secure your account, and to confirm you are old enough to use the service
Postal address, city, region, country and postcodeTo match families with sitters nearby and to tell a sitter where a booking takes place
Profile photo and personal introductionShown to other users so families and sitters can decide who to work with
Booking details — dates, times, children's age groups, hourly rateTo create, schedule and fulfil bookings
Messages you send to other usersTo deliver in-app chat and to investigate reports of abuse
Payment and billing detailsTo take payment for bookings and to pay sitters
Support messagesTo answer your questions

We do not collect your device's location, we do not use analytics or advertising software, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Legal basis

We process your data to perform our contract with you (running the service and its bookings), to meet legal obligations (tax and payment records), and for our legitimate interest in keeping the service safe and preventing abuse.

Who we share it with

We share data only with the providers that make the app work:

  • Stripe — processes payments and sitter payouts
  • Cloudinary — stores and delivers profile photos
  • Vercel — hosts the service that stores your account data

We never sell your personal data, and we never share it with data brokers or advertisers. Information you choose to put on your public profile — your photo and introduction — is visible to other Quick Watch users.

How long we keep it

We keep your account data for as long as your account is open. If you delete your account we remove your personal data within 30 days, except records we must keep by law, such as payment records retained for up to seven years.

Your rights

You can ask us to give you a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, restrict how we use it, or object to our use of it. Email us and we will respond within one month. If you are in the UK or EU and you are unhappy with our response, you may complain to your national data protection authority — in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Children

Quick Watch is for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Booking details include the age group of the children being cared for, supplied by their parent or guardian; we do not create accounts for children.

Security

We protect your data with encrypted connections and access controls. No service can promise perfect security, so please choose a strong, unique password and tell us at once if you think your account has been accessed by someone else.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page and, where the change is significant, notify you in the app.
